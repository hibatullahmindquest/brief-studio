# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Showing empathy toward other contributors
- Giving and accepting useful feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or insulting comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing private information of others
- Any conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove or reject contributions that violate this code.

## Scope

This code applies within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Reporting

If you experience unacceptable behavior, please open an issue titled "moderation" and include relevant details.
